## 1. What changes, in one paragraph
The game becomes a **third-person 3D game over one night on a stretch of East Coast Road** (Shriram), not one pool deck.
Hari can walk out of the party, down to the beach and the fishing village, and **drive the SUV along ECR** (Shriram) to the
tea stall, the petrol bunk, the police checkpoint and the bend where the crash happens. The night is full of people who
each have **a story, a secret, a schedule and something they will or won't tell you** (Shriram: "each one tells a
story"). What you learn from them, and whom you help, leads to **different outcomes and different endings** (Shriram).
The loop gets three new rules that stop it being solvable in loop 2 (Claude, proposed): **fixes collide**, **some
people lie**, and **every loop costs Hari something**.

## 2. Why the old night was solvable in loop 2 (Claude)
1. The tragedy was one straight chain with one fix per link.
2. Everything Hari overheard was true and complete.
3. Fixes had no cost: keeping the keys was a pure win.
4. Hari gave up nothing until the final loop.
5. Nobody reacted to Hari; they waited for the right button.

## 3. The map: ECR at night (Shriram: drive the road; Claude, proposed: the places)
A "dense small world", not a city: about **2 km of ECR** plus the beach, so every place has people and secrets in it.

| Zone | What's there | Who's there (see §5) |
|---|---|---|
| **The villa** | gate and driveway, pool deck, the house (kitchen, guest rooms, **the father's study**, the terrace) | the party, Manikandan, Perumal, Raghavan, Revathi |
| **The private beach** | the Manimekala rock, the fence, the bonfire, Advay's speaker | Advay's crowd, the old woman across the wire |
| **The kuppam** (fishing village) | boats, net-drying ground, Selvam's house, the old woman's hut, the village's small Kadal Amma shrine | Selvam, Muthu, Jothi, Kannan, Ponnamma paati |
| **ECR, the drive** | the villa gate → **Murugan's tea stall** (open all night) → **the petrol bunk** → **the police checkpoint** → **the casuarina bend** (the crash site) → the turn to the **government hospital** | Murugan, Ilango, SI Kalaiselvi, the bunk boy |
| **The hospital** (later loops) | casualty ward at 5:40 AM | the night duty doctor; where Krish ends up if burned |

The highway is no longer only a sound (v1 rule): it becomes a place you can drive. **The crash itself is still never
shown head-on** (Shriram's v1 rule, kept): you reach the bend just before or just after.

## 4. New loop rules (Claude, proposed)
1. **Fixes collide.** Every fix changes the night somewhere else. Examples:
   - Keep the keys with Manikandan → Advay doesn't drive at 5:30, but he **walks drunk to the kuppam** to "settle it",
     there's a fight, and **SI Kalaiselvi arrests Selvam**. With Selvam in the jeep, nobody pulls Muthu out at 5:15.
   - Hide the rocket box → Advay's crowd finds **Diwali leftovers in the SUV's boot**, but now they're fired from the
     driveway at 4:20, and the sparks land on the **guest-room curtains**, not the boats.
   - Call the police at 1 AM → they come at 2:10, see the village boys at the fence and **blame the village**;
     Raghavan gets the "unrest" report he wanted (§6).
   The solution is a **combination**, found across loops, not one button.
2. **Some people lie, and some are wrong.** Clues can contradict. Hari has to find out who is lying and why (§6).
3. **Every loop costs Hari.** Manimekala gives the night back, but not for free:
   - Each loop **starts later** (8:00 → 8:20 → 8:45 → 9:15 …), so early events start slipping out of reach.
   - Hari wakes **colder and wetter**; after a few loops he coughs up seawater in front of people, who start avoiding him.
   - There are **about eight nights**. If they run out, the game ends in "The drowned" (§8).
4. **Trust resets every loop; knowledge doesn't** (v1 rule, kept). Hari has to re-earn each person's trust every night,
   quickly, using what he remembers about them, which is why their personal stories matter.
5. **The referral is a real temptation.** Raghavan (§5) can actually get Hari the job. Helping him has real rewards and
   real costs.

Their job is to make the night feel full and real, and to give the loop a human texture: they say the same things every

## 5b. The love track: Disha (Shriram: "hari needs a love track … he sees someone and he likes her … that outcome happens only if you go talk to her, let's call that girl Disha")
*Disha's name and the rule that the romance only happens if Hari talks to her: Shriram. Who she is and how it plays out:
Claude, proposed.*

**Who she is.** Disha, 22, a final-year architecture student from another college, who came with Nila. She doesn't
belong to the rich crowd either. She spends the party **sketching**: the glass villa, the pool, and then, more and more, the
village boats across the wire. She's the only guest who walks to the fence on her own to look at the other side.

**How Hari meets her.** He sees her at 8:30 PM on the deck steps, sketchbook on her knee. Nothing happens unless the
player chooses to walk over and talk to her (Shriram's rule). If you never do, she leaves by cab at 1 AM, and the night
plays out without her.

**The loop makes it bittersweet.** Every night, Disha meets Hari for the first time; only he remembers. So the romance is
about **learning her**: what she's drawing, why she chose architecture, the house in Madurai her grandfather built, the song
she hums. Each thing Hari remembers lets him reach her a little faster the next night. Get it wrong (fake interest, or
quoting her own words back too obviously) and she pulls away for that loop.

**It costs time.** Every minute with Disha is a minute not spent saving someone. The player chooses.

**It matters to the story, without gating it:**
- If she trusts Hari, she shows him her sketch from 11 PM: **men carrying cable to the fence**. That's a second way to the
  wired-fence clue (Revathi is the first). The love track opens a path; it's never the only path.
- **If you talk to her, she stays past 1 AM**, and that has a cost: in a loop where Hari doesn't stop it, she accepts
  Advay's offer of a ride at 5:30 and is **in the SUV at the bend**. Talking to her puts her in the night's danger, which
  makes protecting her part of the puzzle. *(Claude, proposed; it follows Shriram's rule: she's only at risk if you talk to her.)*
- Around the third loop, she notices Hari's damp shirt and the sand in his pocket, and she's the first guest to ask
  *"are you okay?"* and mean it.

**Outcomes (only if Hari talked to her):**
- **In the true ending:** at dawn she's at the rock with Ponnamma paati, sketching the open beach. After Hari calls Amma,
  Disha sits beside him on the overturned boat. It's the first morning she remembers him, because it's the first morning
  that doesn't reset. She asks his name; he tells her again.
- **In the Hollow Dawn:** she sees Hari standing with Raghavan as the village is blamed, and walks away. Hari has the
  referral and not her.
- **In The Witness:** she gives him her number; whether they meet again is left open.
- **If Hari never talked to her:** she isn't in any ending. Nobody tells the player what they missed.

## 6. The second mystery: who really started the fire (Claude, proposed)
v1's chain said the rocket that burned the nets was Advay's. **In this revision that's what everyone believes, and it's
only half true.** Advay fires his rockets into the sky. The one that lands on the nets was aimed there by **Raghavan's
security man**, so the village "riot" and the police report happen before the 7 AM sealing.
- **Loops 1–3:** the player believes it was Advay. Stopping Advay doesn't stop the fire, which is the shock that ends
  the "solved in loop 2" feeling.
- **Clues:** Revathi saw the cable; Perumal's radio log; Nila's timestamps; Manikandan's silence.
- **Manikandan is morally grey** (Claude, proposed): he suspects Raghavan's plan and stays quiet, because the family pays
  his daughter's college fees. Getting him to speak costs him his job, which is a real choice for Hari.
- **Advay stays what v1 says he is** (Shriram): proud, drunk, not a villain. The engineered fire makes him a scapegoat
  as well as a cause.

## 7. Driving (Shriram: drive the road; Claude, proposed: how it matters)
- **Getting the keys:** from Manikandan with trust; from Advay's pocket when he's drunk; or Raghavan hands them over if
  Hari works for him.
- **What driving opens:** take burned Krish to the hospital yourself; chase Advay; fetch SI Kalaiselvi early; bring
  Kannan's proof to the officials before 7; take Revathi home safely after her shift (she then trusts you next loop).
- **Risks:** Hari has only a two-wheeler licence; the checkpoint stops a middle-class boy in a rich family's SUV; and
  the bend at dawn is where the crash happens: **driving can save someone, or Hari can become the crash**.
- **Scope rule:** a short, dense road with stops, not open-world traffic or chases.

## 8. Endings (Claude, proposed; v1's ending kept as the true one)
The loop ends at any dawn when **no one is lost, Hari included** (v1 rule). That leaves room for good and bad versions:
1. **Before Dawn (true ending, v1's, Shriram):** Vastav opens the fence himself, Raghavan's plan is exposed by Kannan and
   SI Kalaiselvi with Nila's footage, Ponnamma paati touches the rock, Hari calls Amma and tells the truth. Hari loses the
   referral and misses the exam.
2. **The Hollow Dawn:** everyone lives, but Hari helped Raghavan. He gets the referral letter; the village is blamed, the
   court stays the sealing, the fence stays. The loop ends because no one died, and that is the problem.
3. **The Witness:** everyone lives and the fence opens by law, but Manikandan is fired and blamed, and Vastav never
   changes. Bittersweet.
4. **The Drowned:** the nights run out. Hari wakes on the sand at dawn beside his own chappal, and Ponnamma paati closes
   his eyes. (A game-over that is also a story.)

| Endings | branching on loop state | small once the systems exist |

**Plan in phases** (to be lined up with Assignments 4–10 once their briefs are known):
**Phase 1:** 3D third-person Hari, walking the villa and beach, with 4–5 speaking characters. **Phase 2:** the SUV and the
ECR stretch. **Phase 3:** the kuppam, the second mystery, colliding fixes. **Phase 4:** cutscenes and the four endings.
